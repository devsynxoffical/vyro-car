Vyro-Car Trial - See It On Your Car (trial build for topperformance.qa by DEVSYNX)

index.html              Light theme studio. Open directly in a browser, images load from cars/.
cars/                   16 demo vehicles, 1600px JPG + thumbnails (AI studio renders, Nano Banana Pro)
source-renders-2k/      Original 2752x1536 PNG renders
wheel-overlays/         Keyed wheel faces cut from store's product photos (Weld Laguna 6, Weld Solana Drag, Vossen HF6-3, fifteen52 Neito, fifteen52 Analog)
vehicles-manifest.json  Per vehicle: hub coordinates (px in the 1600px image), tire radius, rim radius, stock rim size, bolt pattern
dark-version-v1.html    First dark-theme version with the drawn demo SUV (self-contained)

Hub coordinates and radii are editable live in index.html under "Adjust wheel position".
Wheel fits cover 6x139.7 and 5x150 bolt patterns.
